How to check your DGCA result, admit card and exam dates

  • DGCA exam result: results for each regular session and OLODE session come as a "Declaration of Result" notice above. Open the notice, and also check your account on the Pariksha portal, where you log in with your computer number.
  • Admit card: when the notice "Download of Admit Cards" or "Availability of Admit Cards" appears, log in to Pariksha and download it. It shows your exam date, time and centre.
  • Exam dates and applying: each session's public notice gives the dates the portal is open for applications and the exam dates. They are in the key dates box above when we can read them.
  • Venue list and instructions: read both before the exam day. They list the centres and what to carry.
