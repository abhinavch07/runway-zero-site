The RTR(A), Radio Telephony Restricted (Aeronautical), is the radio operator's certificate every commercial pilot needs. Here is how it works now that the DGCA runs it.

Why you need it

To get a CPL you must hold a current Flight Radio Telephone Operator's Licence (FRTOL). The RTR(A) is the exam behind it: it shows you can use the aircraft radio and the standard phraseology correctly.

Who conducts it

  • The DGCA took over the RTR certificate and licence exams and issue from 1 December 2025, under the Radio Telephone Operator (Restricted) Certificate and Licence Rules, 2025. Before that it was the WPC Wing of the Department of Telecommunications.
  • The DGCA said RTR certificates and licences issued or renewed by WPC would be accepted only until 15 July 2026. For anything after that, follow the DGCA's procedures.
  • RTR sessions are announced with the flight crew exams on the Pariksha portal.

Part 1 and Part 2

PartWhat it tests
Part 1, writtenRadio telephony rules and procedures, and basic radio theory.
Part 2, practicalSpeaking and listening: making the right calls, reading back clearances, numbers, callsigns, emergencies.

Check before you pay

The number of questions, pass marks and fees are in the DGCA's RTR notice for each session. Read it before you apply.

How to prepare

  • Learn the phonetic alphabet and how numbers are spoken: "TREE" for 3, "FIFE" for 5, "NIN-er" for 9, and "decimal" in frequencies.
  • Learn which items must always be read back: runway, level, heading, speed, squawk, frequency and clearances.
  • Practise out loud. Our free RTR Part 2 trainer plays the controller, listens to your call and marks it against the model call, with VFR, IFR and emergency scenarios.
  • Revise the ATS, signals and procedures modules in our Air Regulations notes.

Remember

In short

  • RTR(A) leads to the FRTOL, which the CPL needs.
  • Run by the DGCA since 1 December 2025.
  • Part 1 written, Part 2 practical speaking.
  • Practise calls out loud, not just on paper.

Questions

Who conducts the RTR(A) exam now?

The DGCA, since 1 December 2025. It was earlier conducted by the WPC Wing of the Department of Telecommunications.

Is RTR(A) needed for the CPL?

Yes. The CPL needs a current Flight Radio Telephone Operator's Licence, which needs the RTR(A).

How do I practise Part 2?

Say the calls out loud with someone playing ATC, or use our free RTR Part 2 trainer, which marks each call.
