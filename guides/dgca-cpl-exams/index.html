How the exam works

  • The papers are online multiple choice tests, taken at exam centres and booked on the Pariksha portal.
  • The pass mark is 70% in each paper, with no negative marking.
  • The DGCA runs regular sessions through the year, and online on demand (OLODE) sessions at a higher fee. Dates and venues are in the notices on Pariksha.
  • See the latest DGCA notices for current sessions, results and admit cards.
  • The number of questions and the time allowed differ by paper and are shown in each session's instructions.
  • The fee for the regular flight crew online exam is ₹2,500 per paper (as of November 2026). It is not refunded.

Check before you pay

Pass marks, fees and session rules are set by the DGCA and can change. Read the latest notice on Pariksha before you apply.

Getting your computer number

The computer number is your unique ID with the DGCA's exam office. You can have only one, and it is valid for life. One number covers every flight crew exam.

  1. Register as a new candidate on Pariksha.

    Use an email you check. The activation link expires after 24 hours.

  2. Fill in the form and upload your documents.

    Photo and signature as JPEG, documents as PDF: Class 10 certificate and mark sheet (date of birth), Class 12 mark sheet and pass certificate with Physics and Maths, and ID. Check every upload: you cannot add files after the final submit.

  3. Wait for approval.

    Since 16 October 2025 the DGCA allots the number automatically, in real time, when your Class 10 and 12 records can be checked on DigiLocker. This started with CBSE students and is being extended to other boards. Others are checked by hand.

  4. If it is rejected

    The reasons come by email. Log in, fix them and apply again.

How to prepare

  • Read the notes for one module, then do its practice questions until you score well above 70%.
  • Learn the numbers by heart with flashcards: ages, hours, validity periods, separation minima.
  • Take full timed mock tests before you book. Our mocks keep a score per module, so you can see what to revise.
  • For Air Navigation, practise the sums on a flight computer until they are quick.

Questions

What is the pass mark in the DGCA exams?

70% in each paper, with no negative marking. Check the current notice on Pariksha, as the DGCA sets it.

How many computer numbers can I have?

Only one. It is valid for life and covers every DGCA flight crew exam.

What is OLODE?

Online On Demand Examination: extra DGCA exam sessions at a higher fee, besides the regular sessions.

Which paper should I start with?

Many cadets start with Meteorology or Air Regulations, which need less maths, and do Air Navigation once they are comfortable with the flight computer.
