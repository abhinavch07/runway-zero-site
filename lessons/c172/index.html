Light aircraft with a glass cockpit: start-up, PFD and MFD, run-up, shutdown. 12 guided lessons for this light aircraft, each with a short quiz at the end.

  1. 1

    Before start and engine start

    From a cold aircraft to the engine running at 1000 RPM: fuel, master, beacon, mixture, priming with the fuel pump, the key, then avionics.

    About 6 minutes · 16 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  2. 2

    PFD and MFD basics

    With the engine running: read the glass PFD (speed, attitude, altitude, heading) and the MFD engine page, set the baro and the heading bug.

    About 6 minutes · 8 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  3. 3

    Radios and ATC clearance

    Engine running: tune and swap COM frequencies, listen to the ATIS, set the QNH, get a clearance from Ground and read it back, then set the transponder.

    About 8 minutes · 10 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  4. 4

    VOR and ILS

    Engine running at Delhi: tune and identify a VOR, find which radial you are on (FROM), the course to fly to it (TO), then tune the ILS for runway 28 and read the localizer needle.

    About 8 minutes · 9 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  5. 5

    Run-up before take-off

    At the run-up area: brakes, mixture, flight controls, fuel, trim, 1800 RPM, magneto check, engine and electrics, idle check, ready for take-off.

    About 6 minutes · 14 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  6. 6

    First flight 1: Taxi and take-off

    Hand fly with the keyboard and the view out of the window: taxi to the holding point, line up on runway 28, take off, climb at 70 knots and level off at circuit height.

    About 8 minutes · 10 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  7. 7

    First flight 2: Fly the circuit

    Already flying at circuit height past the end of runway 28: turn crosswind, downwind, base and final, with the before-landing checks and the first stage of flap.

    About 10 minutes · 9 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  8. 8

    First flight 3: Land

    On final for runway 28 with flaps 20: hold 65 knots, keep the slope with the PAPI, power to idle over the runway, flare, touch down and stop.

    About 5 minutes · 6 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  9. 9

    Engine failure after take-off

    Climbing out of runway 28 at 500 ft, the engine stops. Lower the nose at once, glide at 68 knots, land ahead in a field, secure the engine and stop.

    About 5 minutes · 6 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  10. 10

    Alternator failure

    Engine running with lights and pitot heat on when the alternator fails: LOW VOLTS, read the volts and amps, reset the alternator, switch it off, shed electrical load, plan to land.

    About 6 minutes · 7 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  11. 11

    Fuel management and starvation

    Engine running on the LEFT tank, which is almost empty while the right tank is full. See fuel starvation stop the engine, then switch tanks, use the fuel pump and restart.

    About 6 minutes · 6 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  12. 12

    Shutdown and secure

    Parked with the engine running: avionics off, lights off, mixture to idle cut-off, magnetos off, master off, fuel selector to one tank.

    About 4 minutes · 9 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
