C172 · Lesson 2 · about 6 minutes

PFD and MFD basics

Share on WhatsApp

With the engine running: read the glass PFD (speed, attitude, altitude, heading) and the MFD engine page, set the baro and the heading bug.

Start this lesson in the cockpitC172 checklists

The steps

  1. The PFD layout

    Left: airspeed tape. Middle: attitude (blue sky, brown ground). Right: altitude tape, with vertical speed beside it. Bottom: the heading (HSI) rose. Press Next.

  2. The airspeed tape colours

    White band: flaps can be used (up to 85 kt). Green: normal operating speeds. Yellow: smooth air only. Red line: never exceed (163 kt). On the ground it shows no speed yet. Press Next.

  3. BARO: set QNH 1008

    The airfield gives today's pressure: 1008 hPa. Use the BARO − button beside the PFD until it shows 1008. The altitude then reads the airfield elevation, about 780 ft.

  4. HDG bug: set 280

    The cyan heading bug on the HSI marks the heading you want. Set it to 280, the runway heading for runway 28. Hold Shift for 10° steps.

  5. The MFD engine strip

    On the left of the MFD: RPM at the top, then fuel flow, oil pressure, oil temperature and exhaust gas temperature (EGT). Green is normal, red is a limit. Press Next.

  6. Fuel and electrics on the MFD

    Lower on the strip: fuel in each tank (L and R, in gallons), then the bus volts (M BUS, E BUS) and battery current (M BATT, S BATT). Press Next.

  7. THROTTLE: set 1200 RPM and watch

    Open the throttle a little. On the MFD watch the RPM rise and the fuel flow go up with it.

  8. THROTTLE: back to 1000 RPM

    Back to a steady 1000 RPM. Fuel flow comes down again.
