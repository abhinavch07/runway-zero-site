C172 · Lesson 1 · about 6 minutes

Before start and engine start

Share on WhatsApp

From a cold aircraft to the engine running at 1000 RPM: fuel, master, beacon, mixture, priming with the fuel pump, the key, then avionics.

Start this lesson in the cockpitC172 checklists

The steps

  1. Parking brake: SET

    The aircraft must not roll when the engine starts. Check the brake is set.

  2. FUEL SHUTOFF valve: ON

    The red fuel shutoff knob on the floor is pushed in for ON. Pulled out, no fuel can reach the engine.

  3. FUEL SELECTOR: BOTH

    For start, taxi and take-off the selector is on BOTH, so fuel comes from both wing tanks.

  4. AVIONICS: check OFF

    The avionics stay off during engine start. The starter pulls a lot of current and the voltage dips.

  5. MASTER switch: ON (BAT and ALT)

    The master switch has two halves: BAT connects the battery, ALT the alternator. The PFD comes on. On the lower panel the bus shows about 24 V from the battery.

  6. STBY BATT: ARM

    The standby battery keeps the PFD powered if the main electrical system fails. (On the real aircraft you test it first, then arm it.)

  7. BEACON: ON

    The flashing beacon warns people outside that the engine is about to start.

  8. MIXTURE: RICH, THROTTLE: open a little

    Push the mixture fully in (RICH). Open the throttle about a quarter of an inch: one or two presses of + from idle.

  9. FUEL PUMP: ON for 3 to 5 seconds, then OFF

    This engine has fuel injection. Running the electric fuel pump for a few seconds primes it for a cold start. Too long floods the engine.

  10. Propeller area: CLEAR

    Look all around the propeller. On the real aircraft you open the window and call "Clear prop!" before you start. Press Next.

  11. MAGNETOS: hold START, let go when it starts

    Press and hold START on the key. The starter turns the engine. When the RPM rises past about 600, let go: the key springs back to BOTH.

  12. OIL PRESSURE: check it rises

    Within 30 seconds the oil pressure must rise. If it does not, stop the engine at once (mixture to idle cut-off).

  13. THROTTLE: set 1000 RPM

    1000 RPM is a good warm-up speed. Watch the RPM on the lower panel or the MFD as you move the throttle.

  14. AVIONICS: ON

    With the engine running and the voltage steady, the avionics can come on. The MFD starts up.

  15. Check the alternator is charging

    On the lower panel (or the MFD): the bus is now about 28 V and the battery current is positive (charging). Press Next.

  16. NAV lights: ON

    Navigation lights on. The aircraft is ready for the PFD and MFD lesson or the run-up.
