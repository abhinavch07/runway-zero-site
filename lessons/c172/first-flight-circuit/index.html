First flight 2: Fly the circuit

Share on WhatsApp

Already flying at circuit height past the end of runway 28: turn crosswind, downwind, base and final, with the before-landing checks and the first stage of flap.

Start this lesson in the cockpitC172 checklists

The steps

  1. You are flying: wings level, keep the height

    Runway 28 is behind you. You are at circuit height, 1800 ft, about 85 knots. The pink dots on the ground show the circuit: a rectangle around the runway, with every turn to the left. The pink box shows what to aim for.

  2. Turn left onto crosswind, heading 195

    When the pink dots turn left, hold the Left arrow to bank about 20 degrees, let go, and keep a little back pressure (Down arrow) so the nose does not drop. Roll the wings level (Right arrow) as the heading comes to 195.

  3. Turn left onto downwind, heading 105

    When you reach the next line of dots, turn left again to heading 105. The runway is now out of your left window, about a nautical mile away, pointing the other way.

  4. Downwind: hold 1800 ft and 85 knots

    Keep the height with small pitch changes, and trim (Q, E) so you do not have to hold the arrows. Keep the runway the same distance away on your left.

  5. Before landing: LANDING light ON

    The short before-landing check: fuel BOTH and mixture rich are already set. Switch the landing light on. The aircraft is trimmed and keeps flying while you look down.

  6. Abeam the numbers: 1700 RPM, flaps 10 (S, F)

    When the start of the runway is beside you on the left, reduce power (S) to about 1700 RPM and press F once for flaps 10. Keep the nose so the speed settles at 75 knots. The aircraft starts a gentle descent.

  7. Turn left onto base, heading 015

    When the runway threshold is about 45 degrees behind your left shoulder and the dots turn left, turn left to heading 015. Keep 75 knots and keep descending.

  8. Flaps 20, 70 knots (F)

    Press F for flaps 20. The nose wants to rise: hold it so the speed settles at 70 knots. Add a little power (W) if you are getting low.

  9. Turn left onto final, heading 285

    Start the turn a little before the runway centreline, so you roll out lined up with the runway, heading 285. The four PAPI lights left of the runway show the slope: two white and two red is just right.
