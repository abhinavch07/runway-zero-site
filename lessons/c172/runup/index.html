Run-up before take-off

Share on WhatsApp

At the run-up area: brakes, mixture, flight controls, fuel, trim, 1800 RPM, magneto check, engine and electrics, idle check, ready for take-off.

Start this lesson in the cockpitC172 checklists

The steps

  1. Parking brake: SET

    Hold the aircraft still during the run-up. Point it into wind if you can.

  2. MIXTURE: RICH

    At low airfields like Delhi the mixture stays fully rich for the run-up and take-off.

  3. Flight controls: free and correct

    Move the yoke fully forward, back, left and right and check the control surfaces move the right way. (Not modelled here.) Press Next.

  4. FUEL SELECTOR: BOTH

    Fuel from both tanks for take-off.

  5. TRIM: set for take-off

    The trim is nose up from the last landing. Use the trim buttons until the indicator is at the TAKEOFF mark.

  6. THROTTLE: 1800 RPM

    The engine checks are done at 1800 RPM.

  7. MAGNETOS: L, note the RPM drop

    Turn the key to L: the engine runs on the left magneto only. The RPM drops a little. The drop must not be more than 150 RPM.

  8. MAGNETOS: BOTH

    Back to BOTH. The RPM comes back up.

  9. MAGNETOS: R, note the RPM drop

    Now the right magneto only. Again not more than 150 RPM drop, and not more than 50 RPM different from the left.

  10. MAGNETOS: BOTH

    Back to BOTH. Never take off on one magneto.

  11. Engine and electrics: check

    On the MFD: oil pressure and temperature in the green, both bus volts about 28 V, battery current positive. Press Next.

  12. THROTTLE: idle, check it runs smoothly

    Pull the throttle to idle. The engine must keep running smoothly at low RPM.

  13. THROTTLE: 1000 RPM

    Back to 1000 RPM to wait at the holding point.

  14. FLAPS: 0° to 10° for take-off

    A normal take-off uses flaps 0° to 10°.
