PA-28 cockpit lessons

Share on WhatsApp

Low-wing light aircraft with a glass cockpit: hand primer, left and right tanks, run-up, fuel pump failure. 7 guided lessons for this light aircraft, each with a short quiz at the end.

  1. 1

    Before start and engine start

    From a cold aircraft to the engine running at 1000 RPM: fuel selector, master, electric fuel pump, mixture, the hand primer, the key, then avionics and a fuel pressure check.

    About 7 minutes · 17 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  2. 2

    Radios and ATC clearance

    Engine running: tune and swap COM frequencies, listen to the ATIS, set the QNH, get a clearance from Ground and read it back, then set the transponder.

    About 8 minutes · 10 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  3. 3

    VOR and ILS

    Engine running at Delhi: tune and identify a VOR, find which radial you are on (FROM), the course to fly to it (TO), then tune the ILS for runway 28 and read the localizer needle.

    About 8 minutes · 9 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  4. 4

    Run-up before take-off

    At the run-up area: brakes, mixture, controls, take-off tank, electric fuel pump, trim, 2000 RPM, magneto check, engine and fuel pressure, idle check, flaps up.

    About 7 minutes · 16 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  5. 5

    Switching fuel tanks

    Engine running on the LEFT tank, which is low, with a full RIGHT tank. Switch tanks the safe way: electric pump on, firm selection, check the pressure, pump off.

    About 5 minutes · 6 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  6. 6

    Engine fuel pump failure

    Engine running when the engine-driven fuel pump fails: FUEL PRESS warning, electric pump on at once, check the pressure, keep the pump on and plan to land.

    About 5 minutes · 6 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  7. 7

    Shutdown and secure

    Parked with the engine running: avionics off, lights off, mixture to idle cut-off, magnetos off, master off, fuel selector OFF.

    About 4 minutes · 10 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
