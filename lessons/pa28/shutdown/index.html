PA-28 · Lesson 7 · about 4 minutes

Shutdown and secure

Share on WhatsApp

Parked with the engine running: avionics off, lights off, mixture to idle cut-off, magnetos off, master off, fuel selector OFF.

Start this lesson in the cockpitPA-28 checklists

The steps

  1. Parking brake: SET

    Set the brake as soon as you stop at the parking spot.

  2. FUEL PUMP: OFF

    The electric pump was on for landing. Switch it off now you are parked.

  3. THROTTLE: 1000 RPM

    Let the engine run at 1000 RPM for a moment before shutdown.

  4. AVIONICS: OFF

    Avionics off before the engine stops, to protect them from voltage changes.

  5. TAXI light: OFF

    Switch off the lights you no longer need.

  6. MIXTURE: IDLE CUT-OFF

    Pull the mixture fully out. The engine stops because no fuel reaches the cylinders. This is the normal way to stop it.

  7. MAGNETOS: OFF

    Turn the key to OFF and take it out. A magneto left on can start the engine if someone turns the propeller.

  8. BEACON and NAV lights: OFF

    The engine has stopped, so the beacon can go off.

  9. STBY BATT: OFF, MASTER: OFF

    Standby battery off, then both halves of the master switch. Everything goes dark.

  10. FUEL SELECTOR: OFF

    Many schools turn the selector OFF when the aircraft is parked, so no fuel can leak to the engine. Follow your school's checklist.
