Switching fuel tanks

Share on WhatsApp

Engine running on the LEFT tank, which is low, with a full RIGHT tank. Switch tanks the safe way: electric pump on, firm selection, check the pressure, pump off.

Start this lesson in the cockpitPA-28 checklists

The steps

  1. Look at the fuel

    On the MFD: LEFT tank almost empty (and LOW FUEL L on the PFD), RIGHT tank 20 gallons. The selector is on LEFT. There is no BOTH: the engine only gets fuel from the tank you select. Press Next.

  2. FUEL PUMP: ON

    Switch the electric pump on before changing tanks. It keeps the fuel pressure up while the selector moves, and helps if the new line has air in it.

  3. FUEL SELECTOR: RIGHT

    Move the selector to the full tank, firmly into its position. Look at it as you do it: OFF is on the same selector, and selecting OFF by mistake stops the engine.

  4. Check the fuel pressure and the engine

    FUEL PSI in the green and the engine running smoothly at the same RPM. Wait a little to be sure fuel is coming from the new tank. Press Next.

  5. FUEL PUMP: OFF, check the pressure stays

    Switch the electric pump off. The pressure must stay in the green on the engine-driven pump.

  6. Note the time

    Pilots write down the time of each tank change, so they know when to switch back. Press Next.
