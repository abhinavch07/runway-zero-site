A320neo · Lesson 1 · about 6 minutes

Power up

Share on WhatsApp

From a cold and dark aircraft to the APU powering everything: batteries, ground power, ADIRS, fuel pumps and the APU. Engine start comes in lesson 3.

Start this lesson in the cockpitA320neo checklists

The steps

  1. BAT 1 and BAT 2: ON

    The batteries give DC power. That is enough to light the overhead panel and to start the APU. Check each shows about 25 V or more.

  2. EXT PWR: ON

    Ground power gives AC power, so the screens and fuel pumps work without running the APU yet. Press it when AVAIL shows.

  3. ADIRS: IR 1, IR 3, IR 2 to NAV

    The inertial references must align before the aircraft knows its attitude and heading. Do not move the aircraft while they align. Real alignment takes several minutes. Here it takes 1 minute.

  4. Fuel pumps: all ON

    The wing tank pumps send fuel to the engines under pressure.

  5. NAV lights: ON

    Navigation lights stay on whenever the aircraft is powered. Seat belt signs come later, just before engine start (lesson 3).

  6. Look at the main panel

    With AC power the screens are on. The PFD shows red ATT and HDG flags and the ND shows MAP NOT AVAIL: the ADIRS are still aligning. Press Next when you have had a look.

  7. APU MASTER SW: ON

    Opens the APU air intake flap and powers the APU computer. Wait a few seconds for the flap to open.

  8. APU START: press, wait for AVAIL

    The APU is a small jet engine in the tail. Once running it gives electrical power and the air needed to start the main engines. It takes about 45 seconds. The lower ECAM (on the pedestal) shows the APU page by itself: watch its speed (N) and EGT rise, then AVAIL.

  9. APU BLEED: ON

    Sends APU air to the engine starters (and to the air conditioning).

  10. Wait for ADIRS to finish aligning

    When the ALIGN lights go out the PFD shows attitude and heading and the ND shows the map.

  11. EXT PWR: OFF

    Ground power is disconnected before the aircraft moves. The APU generator now powers the aircraft.
