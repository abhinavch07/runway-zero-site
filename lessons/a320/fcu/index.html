A320neo · Lesson 7 · about 8 minutes

FCU basics

Share on WhatsApp

In cruise with the autopilot on: read the FMA, change speed, heading and altitude, and see what each FCU change does.

Start this lesson in the cockpitA320neo checklists

The steps

  1. Read the FMA

    The top row of the PFD is the Flight Mode Annunciator. Left to right: SPEED (autothrust holds the speed), ALT (holding altitude), HDG (holding heading), and AP1 and A/THR engaged on the right. After every FCU change, check the FMA. Press Next.

  2. Set SPD 220 on the FCU

    Use the + and − buttons or scroll over the value. The cyan number above the speed tape and the cyan triangle move to 220.

  3. Watch the speed reduce to 220 kt

    Autothrust reduces thrust (see N1 drop on the engine display) until the speed reaches the target, then adds thrust to hold it.

  4. Set HDG 090 on the FCU

    In HDG mode the aircraft turns as soon as the heading changes. It turns the shorter way, banking up to 25°. (When following a flight plan in NAV mode you would PULL the knob to take the selected heading.)

  5. Watch the turn on the PFD and ND

    The PFD shows the bank. On the ND the cyan heading bug sits at 090 and the compass turns until it is under the yellow line.

  6. Set ALT 12000 on the FCU

    This is the target altitude. Nothing happens yet: the autopilot must be told how to climb.

  7. Set V/S +1500, then PULL V/S

    Pulling the V/S knob tells the autopilot to climb at the selected rate. The FMA shows V/S in green.

  8. Watch ALT* and then ALT

    Near 12,000 ft the FMA changes to ALT* (capturing) and the climb rate eases off. At the altitude it shows ALT (holding).

  9. Descend: ALT 10000, V/S −1000, PULL V/S

    Same idea downwards. Set the lower altitude first, then the descent rate, then pull.

  10. Wait for ALT at 10,000 ft

    Watch the altitude tape and the FMA again: V/S, then ALT*, then ALT.

  11. Disconnect the autopilot: press AP1

    AP OFF shows in red. In the real aircraft the pilot then flies with the sidestick. Here the aircraft simply holds its attitude.

  12. Engage the autopilot again: press AP1

    AP1 shows in white on the FMA again. With nothing selected it holds the current heading and vertical speed.
