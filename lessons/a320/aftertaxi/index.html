A320neo · Lesson 5 · about 10 minutes

After start and taxi

Share on WhatsApp

Engines running at the gate: ground spoilers, rudder and pitch trim, flaps, autobrake, flight control check, then release the brakes, taxi a short way, check the brakes and do the T.O CONFIG test.

Start this lesson in the cockpitA320neo checklists

The steps

  1. APU BLEED and APU MASTER SW: check OFF

    After engine start the engines give power and air, so the APU is off. Check both on the overhead.

  2. GROUND SPOILERS: ARM

    Armed spoilers come up by themselves if a take-off is rejected or on landing, killing the lift. Move the speedbrake lever to ARM.

  3. RUDDER TRIM: check zero

    The rudder trim shows L 1.5. It must be zero for take-off. Press RESET.

  4. FLAPS: 1

    Set the take-off flaps from the MCDU PERF page: FLAPS 1.

  5. PITCH TRIM: set UP 0.8

    The PERF page says THS UP0.8. Set the pitch trim wheel to match: press NOSE UP until it shows UP 0.8.

  6. AUTO BRK: MAX

    For take-off the autobrake is set to MAX. If the take-off is rejected at high speed, it brakes as hard as it can.

  7. Flight controls: check on the F/CTL page

    Press F/CTL on the ECAM control panel. Then on the main panel move the sidestick fully back, forward, left and right (hold the buttons or use the arrow keys). Watch the surfaces move on the F/CTL page.

  8. HYD page: check pressure

    Press HYD. Green, blue and yellow all show about 3000 psi with both engines running. Anti-ice is not needed today (25°C, dry). Press Next.

  9. TAXI light: ON

    The nose light for taxiing.

  10. Parking brake: OFF

    You have taxi clearance. Release the parking brake.

  11. THRUST: a little, until the aircraft moves

    Press + on the thrust levers three or four times. A heavy aircraft starts to roll slowly. Use only a little thrust on the ground.

  12. Brake check: thrust IDLE, then hold BRAKES

    Thrust levers to IDLE, then hold BRAKES (toe brakes) until the aircraft stops. This checks the brakes work before take-off.

  13. T.O CONFIG: press

    The T.O CONFIG button tests the take-off configuration as if take-off thrust were set. The upper ECAM should show T.O CONFIG NORMAL. If it shows a red CONFIG message, fix what it says and test again.

  14. Read the T.O memo

    On the main panel, the upper ECAM shows the T.O memo: AUTO BRK MAX, SIGNS ON, CABIN CHECK, SPLRS ARM, FLAPS T.O, T.O CONFIG TEST. Green items are done, blue items are not. Press Next when all are green.

  15. Parking brake: SET

    You are at the holding point waiting for take-off clearance.
