ECAM: a generator fault

Share on WhatsApp

Engines running at the gate when GEN 1 fails: MASTER CAUT, read the ECAM, do the actions in order (reset the generator, switch it off, start the APU), then clear the ECAM.

Start this lesson in the cockpitA320neo checklists

The steps

  1. Wait: something is about to fail

    Look at the main panel and listen. In a few seconds a fault will appear.

  2. MASTER CAUT: press to silence

    The amber MASTER CAUT light flashes on the glareshield with a single chime. Press it: the light goes out. That only acknowledges the caution, it fixes nothing.

  3. Read the ECAM

    The upper ECAM shows ELEC GEN 1 FAULT in amber with the actions in blue below it. The lower ECAM (pedestal) shows the ELEC page with GEN 1 in amber. On the overhead, GEN 1 shows FAULT. Press Next.

  4. GEN 1: OFF then ON

    The first action line: try to reset the generator. Press GEN 1 on the overhead (OFF), then press it again (ON). Each action line disappears from the ECAM when it is done.

  5. Still FAULT: GEN 1 OFF

    The reset did not work, so the ECAM now says IF UNSUCCESSFUL: GEN 1 OFF. Switch it off. GEN 2 now powers both sides.

  6. APU: MASTER SW ON, then START

    The last action line: start the APU, so its generator can replace GEN 1. Wait for AVAIL.

  7. Check the ELEC page

    On the pedestal, the ELEC page shows AC 1 now fed by the APU GEN and AC 2 by GEN 2. Press Next.

  8. ECAM: press CLR

    All action lines are done. Press CLR on the ECAM control panel to clear the alert from the upper ECAM.
