A320neo cockpit lessons

Airliner: overhead, MCDU, FCU and engine start, Delhi to Mumbai. 11 guided lessons for this airliner, each with a short quiz at the end.

  1. 1

    Power up

    From a cold and dark aircraft to the APU powering everything: batteries, ground power, ADIRS, fuel pumps and the APU. Engine start comes in lesson 3.

    About 6 minutes · 11 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  2. 2

    MCDU setup: Delhi to Mumbai

    Power up, then set up the flight computer for VIDP to VABB: INIT A, flight plan, INIT B fuel and PERF take-off.

    About 15 minutes · 26 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  3. 3

    Radios and ATC clearance

    At the gate: tune VHF 1 on the radio management panel, listen to the ATIS, get the departure clearance from Delivery and read it back, set the transponder, the cleared altitude and the VOR on the RAD NAV page.

    About 8 minutes · 10 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  4. 4

    Before start and engine start

    At the gate with the MCDU ready for Delhi to Mumbai: flight directors, QNH, cleared altitude, check the PFD and ND, then start both engines.

    About 8 minutes · 16 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  5. 5

    After start and taxi

    Engines running at the gate: ground spoilers, rudder and pitch trim, flaps, autobrake, flight control check, then release the brakes, taxi a short way, check the brakes and do the T.O CONFIG test.

    About 10 minutes · 15 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  6. 6

    Take-off

    Lined up on runway 28: release the brakes, set thrust, 100 knots, V1, rotate, gear up, thrust to CL, autothrust and autopilot, flaps up, level off at 5000 ft.

    About 8 minutes · 14 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  7. 7

    FCU basics

    In cruise with the autopilot on: read the FMA, change speed, heading and altitude, and see what each FCU change does.

    About 8 minutes · 12 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  8. 8

    Fuel imbalance and crossfeed

    In cruise with less fuel in the left wing: read the FUEL page, open the crossfeed, switch off the lighter side's pumps so both engines feed from the right, wait until balanced, then put everything back.

    About 6 minutes · 7 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  9. 9

    ECAM: a generator fault

    Engines running at the gate when GEN 1 fails: MASTER CAUT, read the ECAM, do the actions in order (reset the generator, switch it off, start the APU), then clear the ECAM.

    About 6 minutes · 8 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  10. 10

    Engine fire on the ground

    Engines running at the gate when ENG 1 catches fire: MASTER WARN, read the red ECAM warning, engine master off, push the fire pushbutton, fire both bottles, and decide on an evacuation.

    About 5 minutes · 8 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  11. 11

    Shutdown and secure

    Parked with engines running: set the brake, start the APU, stop the engines and switch the aircraft off.

    About 6 minutes · 12 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
