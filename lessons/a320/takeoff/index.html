A320neo · Lesson 6 · about 8 minutes

Take-off

Share on WhatsApp

Lined up on runway 28: release the brakes, set thrust, 100 knots, V1, rotate, gear up, thrust to CL, autothrust and autopilot, flaps up, level off at 5000 ft.

Start this lesson in the cockpitA320neo checklists

The steps

  1. Lined up on runway 28

    Flaps 2 are set, the PFD shows V1 and V2, and the FCU altitude is 5000 ft. Look at the main panel, then press Next.

  2. Parking brake: OFF

    Take-off clearance received. Release the parking brake.

  3. THRUST: about 50% N1, let both engines settle

    Press + on the thrust levers to about 50%. Watch N1 on the upper ECAM: both engines must settle at the same N1 before you set take-off thrust.

  4. THRUST: FLX/MCT

    Set the levers to the FLX/MCT detent: reduced take-off thrust, from the FLEX temperature on the PERF page. Keep straight on the centreline.

  5. 100 knots: cross-check the speed

    Call "100 knots" and check both speeds agree. Watch the speed tape on the main panel.

  6. V1

    At V1 the decision is made: you will take off, even with a failure. Take your hand off the thrust levers.

  7. ROTATE: hold PULL until airborne, then about 15°

    At VR hold PULL on the sidestick (or the down arrow key). The nose comes up and the aircraft lifts off. Keep holding until it is in the air and the PFD shows about 15° nose up, then let go: in the air the flight controls hold that pitch. On the ground they do not, so do not let go too early.

  8. Positive climb: GEAR UP

    When the altitude and vertical speed are rising, call "positive climb" and select the gear UP.

  9. THRUST: CL

    At the thrust reduction altitude (about 1,500 ft above the runway) bring the levers back to the CL detent.

  10. A/THR: press on the FCU

    Autothrust now controls the thrust to hold the speed. On the aircraft it becomes active by itself when the levers reach CL. Here you press A/THR.

  11. AP1: engage

    Press AP1. AP1 shows in the FMA. Keep watching the speed and altitude.

  12. FCU SPD: 210

    Accelerate to clean up the flaps. Set SPD 210 on the FCU.

  13. FLAPS: 0 above 190 kt

    When the speed passes about 190 kt (the clean-up speed here), retract the flaps to 0.

  14. Watch the level-off at 5000 ft

    The FCU altitude is 5000 ft. The FMA shows ALT* as the aircraft captures it, then ALT. If the aircraft is not climbing, set V/S +1500 and PULL V/S.
