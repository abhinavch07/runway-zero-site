B737 · Lesson 6 · about 8 minutes

VOR and ILS

Share on WhatsApp

At the gate: tune and identify the RZV VOR on NAV 1, use the ND in VOR mode and the MCP COURSE to find your radial and the course to the station, then tune the ILS for runway 28 and read it in APP mode.

Start this lesson in the cockpitB737 checklists

The steps

  1. NAV 1: tune 113.35 (RZV) and swap

    The NAV 1 panel is on the pedestal, next to VHF 1. Tune the standby to 113.35, then press ⇆.

  2. Identify: press ID and listen

    Never use a navaid you have not identified. You hear R Z V in Morse code (.-. --.. ...-).

  3. EFIS: VOR mode

    On the EFIS panel choose VOR. The ND shows a full compass with the course pointer and the deviation bar.

  4. COURSE: centre the bar with FROM

    Turn the MCP COURSE (left end of the MCP, Shift for 10°) until the deviation bar is in the middle and the ND shows FROM.

  5. Read your radial

    With the bar centred and FROM, the course is the radial you are on: about 118. You are about 18 NM south-east of RZV (DME at the top right). Press Next.

  6. COURSE: centre it with TO

    Add 180° (about 298) and centre the bar again. Now it shows TO: the course to fly to the station.

  7. HEADING: set it to the course

    To fly to RZV you would turn to that heading (plus a correction for wind). Set the MCP HEADING to the same number as the COURSE.

  8. NAV 1: tune 110.55 (ILS 28) and swap

    An ILS guides you to the runway. Its localizer shows the centreline and its glide slope the descent path.

  9. EFIS: APP mode, COURSE 285

    Choose APP on the EFIS panel and set the COURSE to 285, the runway 28 localizer course.

  10. Read the localizer

    The bar is far to the right: the runway centreline is to your right. The PFD also shows the localizer scale under the attitude. A localizer is about four times more sensitive than a VOR. Press Next.
