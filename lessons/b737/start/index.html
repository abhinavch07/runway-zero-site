B737 · Lesson 3 · about 10 minutes

Engine start

Share on WhatsApp

With APU power and air: fuel pumps, electric hydraulic pumps, anti-collision light, packs off, then engine 2 and engine 1 (start switch GRD, start lever IDLE at 25% N2), generators on, packs on, APU bleed off.

The steps

  1. FUEL PUMPS: the four wing pumps ON

    L FWD, L AFT, R FWD and R AFT. Their LOW PRESSURE lights go out. The centre tank is empty today, so the CTR pumps stay off.

  2. HYDRAULIC: ELEC 1 and ELEC 2 ON

    The electric pumps pressurise systems A and B before the engines run. The ENG pumps are already on and will work once the engines turn. Watch PRESS A / B rise to 3000.

  3. ANTI COLLISION light: ON

    The red flashing light tells people outside that the engines are about to start.

  4. PACKS: both OFF

    The packs use APU air for air conditioning. Switch them off so all the air goes to the engine starters. DUCT PRESS rises to about 36 psi. With both packs on there is too little for a start.

  5. ENGINE 2 START: GRD

    Engine 2 (right) is usually started first. Turn its start switch to GRD. The start valve opens and air turns the engine. N2 starts to rise (ENGINES box, or the engine display).

  6. At 25% N2: START LEVER 2 to IDLE

    Wait until N2 reaches 25% (watch the ENGINES box). Then move start lever 2 to IDLE. This gives fuel and the igniters light it. Too early and the start can run hot.

  7. Watch the start: EGT, N1, then cut-out

    On the engine display: EGT rises (light-up), then N1. At about 56% N2 the starter cuts out, START VALVE OPEN goes out and the start switch springs back to OFF. The engine settles at idle.

  8. ENGINE 1 START: GRD

    Now the left engine, the same way.

  9. At 25% N2: START LEVER 1 to IDLE

    Wait for 25% N2 on engine 1, then start lever 1 to IDLE.

  10. Wait for engine 1 to settle

    Watch EGT and N1 rise, then the start switch goes back to OFF at about 56% N2.

  11. GEN 1 and GEN 2: ON

    Put each engine's generator on its bus. The GEN OFF BUS lights go out. The APU is no longer powering the buses.

  12. PACKS: both ON

    The engines now give bleed air, so the packs can run again for the cabin.

  13. APU BLEED: OFF

    The engine bleeds supply the air now. Switch the APU bleed off.

  14. APU: OFF

    The APU is no longer needed. (Some airlines keep it running a little longer.) Its EGT falls as it stops.
