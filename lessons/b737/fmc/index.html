B737 · Lesson 2 · about 12 minutes

FMC route set-up

Share on WhatsApp

On the CDU: IDENT, the IRS position, the route Delhi to Mumbai through the training waypoints, ACTIVATE and EXEC, performance data, and the take-off flaps and speeds.

Start this lesson in the cockpitB737 checklists

The steps

  1. IDENT page: check it

    The CDU (on the pedestal) shows the IDENT page: the aircraft model and which navigation data is loaded. Check them first. Press Next.

  2. POS INIT: press the key next to POS INIT>

    The keys beside the screen are line select keys (LSK). Lines are counted 1 to 6 from the top, L on the left and R on the right. POS INIT> is at 6R.

  3. REF AIRPORT: VIDP

    Type V, I, D, P on the keyboard. It appears in the scratchpad at the bottom of the screen. Then press 2L, next to REF AIRPORT.

  4. GPS POS: copy it to the scratchpad

    With the scratchpad empty, press 4R next to GPS POS. The position is copied to the scratchpad.

  5. SET IRS POS: press 5R

    Press 5R to give the copied position to the IRS. This is how they know where they are.

  6. ROUTE>: press 6R

    Go on to the route page (RTE 1).

  7. ORIGIN VIDP and DEST VABB

    Type VIDP and press 1L (ORIGIN). Type VABB and press 1R (DEST).

  8. RUNWAY 28 and FLT NO. RZ456

    Type 28 and press 2L (RUNWAY). Type RZ456 and press 2R (flight number).

  9. NEXT PAGE: RTE page 2

    Page 2 is where the route itself goes, as VIA (how) and TO (where).

  10. Waypoints: RZ101, RZ102, RZ103

    Type RZ101 and press 1R. VIA fills in as DIRECT. Then RZ102 at 2R and RZ103 at 3R. These are made-up training waypoints between Delhi and Mumbai.

  11. ACTIVATE>: press 6R

    The route is ready. ACTIVATE makes it the route to fly. The EXEC key lights up.

  12. EXEC

    Press the lit EXEC key. The title changes to ACT RTE 1: the route is active. EXEC is how the FMC asks you to confirm a change.

  13. PERF INIT>: press 6R

    Next, the performance data: weights, fuel reserve, cost index and cruise altitude.

  14. ZFW: 55.0

    Zero fuel weight is the aircraft with passengers and cargo but no fuel, in tonnes. Type 55.0 and press 3L. The gross weight (GW) at the top is worked out from it and the fuel on board.

  15. RESERVES 2.5 and COST INDEX 30

    Type 2.5 and press 4L (fuel to keep in reserve, tonnes). Type 30 and press 5L. The cost index balances fuel against time: low saves fuel, high saves time.

  16. CRZ ALT: FL350

    Type FL350 (or 350) and press 1R. Flight level 350 is 35,000 ft on the standard pressure setting.

  17. TAKEOFF>: press 6R

    Last page: the take-off reference data.

  18. FLAPS: 5

    Type 5 and press 1L. Flaps 5 is a common take-off setting. The FMC then offers take-off speeds (small, at the right).

  19. V1, VR, V2: press 1R, 2R, 3R

    Press each key to accept the speed. V1 is the decision speed, VR the rotation speed, V2 the safe climb speed with one engine out. These are made-up training values, not real performance data.

  20. Check: PRE-FLT COMPLETE, and the route on the ND

    The CDU shows PRE-FLT COMPLETE. On the main panel the ND shows the route in magenta, from Delhi to RZ101. Press Next.
