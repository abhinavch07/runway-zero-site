PA-28 diesel · Lesson 6 · about 4 minutes

After landing and shutdown

Share on WhatsApp

The engine is already running from the start lesson. Flaps up, fuel pump off, strobe to FIN, transponder 2000, then idle, brake, lights and avionics off, engine master off, battery off.

Start this lesson in the cockpitPA-28 diesel checklists

The steps

  1. FLAPS: UP

    The engine is already running from the start lesson. You have vacated the runway. Flaps up.

  2. ELECTRIC FUEL PUMP: OFF

    No longer needed after landing.

  3. STROBE: FIN

    Once past the holding point, the wing strobes go off so they do not dazzle others. The tail strobe stays on.

  4. XPDR: 2000

    Set the transponder back to 2000. Type 2, 0, 0, 0.

  5. Radio: report runway vacated

    Tell Tower you have vacated the runway. Press Next.

  6. At the parking: THRUST LEVER IDLE, PARKING BRAKE SET

    You have taxied in. Idle and set the brake.

  7. FAN, LANDING LIGHT, NAV LIGHTS: OFF

    Lights and fan off.

  8. EMERGENCY BATTERY and AVIONICS: OFF

    Before the engine stops, so the screens and radios are not hit by the voltage change.

  9. ENGINE MASTER: OFF

    The FADEC stops the fuel and the engine stops.

  10. FIN STROBE: OFF

    The engine has stopped. Strobe off.

  11. MASTER BATTERY and ALTERNATOR: OFF

    Last of all, the power.

  12. Walk-round and fuel check

    Release the parking brake only if the aircraft will be towed and is chocked. Walk round the aircraft and check the fuel left. Press Next.
