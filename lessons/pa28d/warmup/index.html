PA-28 diesel · Lesson 4 · about 6 minutes

Warm-up and FADEC test

Share on WhatsApp

The engine is already running from the start lesson. Warm the engine at idle and 1400 RPM until OT 50 °C and CT 60 °C, run the FADEC test, check FORCE B, then a full power check: load 94% or more, 2240 to 2300 RPM.

Start this lesson in the cockpitPA-28 diesel checklists

The steps

  1. Idle, about 900 RPM: warm up

    The engine is already running from the start lesson. A cold diesel warms at idle first, about 2 minutes on the real aircraft. Here it is sped up. Watch CT and OT start to rise.

  2. THRUST LEVER: 1400 RPM until OT 50 °C and CT 60 °C

    Set about 1400 RPM with the + button. Hold it there until the oil (OT) reaches 50 °C and the coolant (CT) 60 °C. If the engine is already warm, this is quick.

  3. THRUST LEVER: IDLE

    The FADEC test is done at idle.

  4. FADEC lights: check OFF

    FADEC A and FADEC B must both be out before the test.

  5. FADEC TEST: press and HOLD to the end

    Hold the button about 10 seconds and watch: both lights come on and the RPM rises, then only B with a propeller check (RPM drops), then A with the RPM up and down again. When the lights go out the RPM settles about 900 to 920.

  6. FADEC TEST: RELEASE

    Let go of the button. Both lights must stay out.

  7. FORCE B: switch to FORCE B

    Now FADEC B alone runs the engine. This checks the backup computer works.

  8. Engine running normally: CHECK

    The engine must run smoothly on FADEC B, with no change in RPM. Press Next.

  9. FORCE B: AUTO

    Back to AUTO for the flight.

  10. THRUST LEVER: FULL, check load and RPM

    Parking brake set, hold the brakes. Full power: the load must be 94% or more and the RPM 2240 to 2300. Lower means a problem, do not take off.

  11. THRUST LEVER: IDLE

    Back to idle. The run-up is complete.
