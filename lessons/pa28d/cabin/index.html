PA-28 diesel · Lesson 1 · about 5 minutes

Cabin checks

Share on WhatsApp

Before you touch the engine: brake, switches off, battery on to read the fuel and the warning lights, battery off, then controls, flaps, trim and the alternate air door.

Start this lesson in the cockpitPA-28 diesel checklists

The steps

  1. Breath analyser test and documents: DONE

    In India the pre-flight breath analyser test is done before every flight. Also make sure the pitot cover is off, the weight and balance is done and the avionics guide is on board. Press Next.

  2. Parking brake: SET

    The aircraft must not move while you work in the cockpit.

  3. ENGINE MASTER: OFF

    The engine master switches the engine's computer (the FADEC) on. It stays off until you are ready to start.

  4. AVIONICS: OFF

    The radios and screens stay off for now.

  5. Electrical switches: all OFF

    Someone left the NAV lights and the pitot heat on. Switch every light, the fuel pump, the pitot heat and the fan off, so nothing pulls power when the battery comes on.

  6. CABIN HEAT: CLOSED

    Cabin heat is closed for start and take-off. In some fires it must be closed, so close it by habit.

  7. MASTER BATTERY: ON

    Switch BATT on. The PFD and the engine page on the MFD come on from the battery, so you can read the fuel and the lights.

  8. Fuel quantity, left and right: CHECK

    Read FUEL GAL on the engine page. Compare it with what you saw in the tanks on the walk-round. Today both tanks have 20 gallons. Press Next.

  9. Fuel temperature and WATER LEVEL light: CHECK

    FUEL TEMP must be in the green. The WATER LVL light must be out: if it is lit, the engine is short of coolant and you do not fly. Press Next.

  10. Warning lights: CHECK

    Look at the lights under the engine page and the messages on the PFD. Nothing should be lit that you cannot explain. Press Next.

  11. MASTER BATTERY: OFF

    The checks that needed power are done. Switch BATT off to save the battery for the start.

  12. Flight controls: CHECK

    Move the control wheel fully in all directions. Watch the ailerons and elevator move freely and the right way. Press Next.

  13. FLAPS: down in stages to 40°

    The flaps are worked by hand. Take them down one stage at a time, 10°, 25°, 40°, and check each stage holds.

  14. FLAPS: back up to 0°

    Bring the flaps all the way up again.

  15. TRIM: NEUTRAL

    The trim was left nose up. Bring the marker back to the middle (TAKEOFF).

  16. ALTERNATE AIR: CLOSED

    The alternate air door is only for when the normal air intake is blocked. It stays closed.

  17. Drains, windows, tow bar, baggage, fire extinguisher: CHECK

    Pitot and static drains drained and closed, windows clean, tow bar stowed, baggage and baggage door secure, fire extinguisher needle in the green. Press Next.
