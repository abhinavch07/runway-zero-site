PA-28 diesel · Lesson 5 · about 6 minutes

Before taxi and holding point checks

Share on WhatsApp

The engine is already running from the start lesson. Controls, screens, altimeter, heading and transponder before taxi. At the holding point: tank, flaps 25°, trim, briefing, then the delayed items once cleared to line up.

The steps

  1. Flight controls: FREE and CORRECT

    The engine is already running from the start lesson. Full movement in every direction, and the surfaces move the right way. Press Next.

  2. PFD and MFD: no red crosses

    A red cross means that reading has failed. There must be none. Press Next.

  3. ALTIMETER: set QNH 1008

    Set today's QNH with BARO. The altitude then reads about 780 ft, the airfield elevation.

  4. HEADING: compare with the compass

    The PFD heading must agree with the standby compass. Press Next.

  5. XPDR: set 2000

    Until ATC gives you a code, set 2000. Type 2, 0, 0, 0.

  6. At the holding point: PARKING BRAKE SET

    You have taxied to the holding point. Set the brake for the checks.

  7. FUEL SELECTOR: the proper tank

    Left has 15 gallons, right 20. Take off on the fuller tank: RIGHT.

  8. Battery and alternator ON, engine readings and fuel temperature GREEN

    Check BATT and ALTR are on, and every engine reading and FUEL TEMP is in the green. Press Next.

  9. ALTERNATE AIR: CLOSED, seats, belts, doors: SECURE

    Alternate air closed. Seats upright, belts fastened, doors latched.

  10. FLAPS: 25°

    Take-off flap is 25°.

  11. TRIM: NEUTRAL for take-off

    Bring the trim marker to the middle (TAKEOFF).

  12. Take-off briefing: COMPLETE

    Say it out loud: lift off at 50 to 60 kt, climb at 76 kt. Engine failure on the runway: idle and brake. Engine failure after take-off: 73 kt, land ahead, no turning back. Press Next.

  13. Runway and approach: CLEAR, wind sock: CHECK

    Look along the runway and up the approach path for traffic. Note the wind. Press Next.

  14. Cleared to line up. ELECTRIC FUEL PUMP: ON

    These are the delayed items, done once ATC clears you onto the runway. The fuel pump stays on for take-off in case the engine pump fails.

  15. STROBE: ALL, LANDING LIGHT: ON

    Wing strobes and the landing light on, so other traffic can see you on the runway.

  16. XPDR: ALT

    The transponder sends your altitude to ATC from now on.

  17. PARKING BRAKE: RELEASED

    Release the brake. You are ready for the take-off clearance.
