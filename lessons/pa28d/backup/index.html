PA-28 diesel · Lesson 3 · about 5 minutes

FADEC backup battery test and after start

Share on WhatsApp

The engine is already running from the start lesson. Prove the FADEC can run the engine on its own battery: emergency battery, alternator and battery off for 10 seconds, then everything back on, avionics, lights, radios and the QNH.

Start this lesson in the cockpitPA-28 diesel checklists

The steps

  1. EMERGENCY BATTERY: OFF

    The engine is already running from the start lesson. For this test every other source of power goes off, so the only thing left powering the FADEC is its own backup battery.

  2. ALTERNATOR: OFF

    Switch ALTR off.

  3. MASTER BATTERY: OFF for 10 seconds

    Switch BATT off and wait 10 seconds. The screens go dark, but the engine must keep running normally. That proves the FADEC backup battery works.

  4. MASTER BATTERY: ON

    The engine kept running. Power back on: BATT first.

  5. ALTERNATOR: ON

    Then ALTR.

  6. EMERGENCY BATTERY: ON

    The emergency battery keeps the PFD going if the main power fails in flight.

  7. AVIONICS: ON

    Radios and navigation on.

  8. HEADSET: ON, NAV LIGHTS: ON

    Put the headset on and switch the NAV lights on.

  9. BATTERY AMPS positive, VOLTS green

    The alternator is charging again after the test.

  10. COM and NAV frequencies: CHECK

    Check the frequencies at the top of the PFD are the ones you need. Press Next.

  11. PFD instruments: CHECK

    Attitude, speed, altitude, heading and the needles all showing, no red crosses. Press Next.

  12. BARO: set QNH 1008

    Today's QNH is 1008. Use BARO −. The altitude then reads the airfield elevation, about 780 ft.
