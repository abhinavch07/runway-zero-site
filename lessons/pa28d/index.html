PA-28 diesel cockpit lessons

Low-wing trainer with a diesel engine and FADEC: glow and start, backup battery test, warm-up, FADEC test, holding point checks and emergencies. 13 guided lessons for this diesel light aircraft, each with a short quiz at the end.

  1. 1

    Cabin checks

    Before you touch the engine: brake, switches off, battery on to read the fuel and the warning lights, battery off, then controls, flaps, trim and the alternate air door.

    About 5 minutes · 17 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  2. 2

    Before start and engine start

    Fuel selector, thrust lever free, alternator and battery, fin strobe, FORCE B in AUTO, engine master, wait for GLOW, fuel pump, starter, then oil pressure, amps and volts.

    About 6 minutes · 22 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  3. 3

    FADEC backup battery test and after start

    The engine is already running from the start lesson. Prove the FADEC can run the engine on its own battery: emergency battery, alternator and battery off for 10 seconds, then everything back on, avionics, lights, radios and the QNH.

    About 5 minutes · 12 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  4. 4

    Warm-up and FADEC test

    The engine is already running from the start lesson. Warm the engine at idle and 1400 RPM until OT 50 °C and CT 60 °C, run the FADEC test, check FORCE B, then a full power check: load 94% or more, 2240 to 2300 RPM.

    About 6 minutes · 11 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  5. 5

    Before taxi and holding point checks

    The engine is already running from the start lesson. Controls, screens, altimeter, heading and transponder before taxi. At the holding point: tank, flaps 25°, trim, briefing, then the delayed items once cleared to line up.

    About 6 minutes · 17 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  6. 6

    After landing and shutdown

    The engine is already running from the start lesson. Flaps up, fuel pump off, strobe to FIN, transponder 2000, then idle, brake, lights and avionics off, engine master off, battery off.

    About 4 minutes · 12 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  7. 7

    Emergency: engine failure on the take-off roll

    The engine is already running. The engine stops at 35 kt on the runway. Abandon the take-off: idle, brakes, flaps up, engine master, alternator and batteries off.

    About 3 minutes · 7 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  8. 8

    Emergency: engine failure after take-off

    You are already flying with the engine running. The engine stops in the climb. Best glide 73 kt, land ahead, then fuel off, fuel pump off, engine master off, flaps, electrics off, door unlatched.

    About 3 minutes · 9 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  9. 9

    Emergency: engine failure in flight and restart

    You are already flying with the engine running. The engine stops in the cruise. Full power, 65 to 68 kt, the fuller tank, fuel pump on, lever idle, engine master off then on, starter if the propeller has stopped, full power.

    About 4 minutes · 11 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  10. 10

    Emergency: FADEC warning in flight

    You are already flying with the engine running. FADEC A starts flashing. Press FADEC TEST for 2 seconds. It stays on steady: B is running the engine. Watch B, avoid overspeed, land as soon as practical.

    About 3 minutes · 5 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  11. 11

    Emergency: WATER LEVEL light and high coolant temperature

    You are already flying with the engine running. The engine is losing coolant: WATER LVL lights and CT climbs into the red. Less power, more speed, cabin heat closed, watch CT and OT, land as soon as practical.

    About 4 minutes · 6 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  12. 12

    Emergency: engine fire during start

    The engine has just been started. Smoke and flames from the cowling just after start. Engine master off, fuel selector off, fuel pump off, battery off, then the fire is out and you get out.

    About 2 minutes · 7 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
  13. 13

    Emergency: alternator failure

    You are already flying with the engine running. The alternator stops charging. Check amps, recycle the alternator, circuit breaker, battery on, cut the electrical load, alternator off, land as soon as practical.

    About 3 minutes · 6 steps

    See the stepsStart the lesson
