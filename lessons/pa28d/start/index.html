PA-28 diesel · Lesson 2 · about 6 minutes

Before start and engine start

Share on WhatsApp

Fuel selector, thrust lever free, alternator and battery, fin strobe, FORCE B in AUTO, engine master, wait for GLOW, fuel pump, starter, then oil pressure, amps and volts.

Start this lesson in the cockpitPA-28 diesel checklists

The steps

  1. Seats, belts, door and briefing: DONE

    Pre-flight inspection complete, pitot cover removed, passenger briefing given, seat upright and locked, door latched, belts fastened. Press Next.

  2. Parking brake: SET

    Check the brake is set before the engine starts.

  3. FUEL SELECTOR: the tank you want

    There is a tank in each wing and no BOTH position. The selector was left OFF. Pick LEFT or RIGHT. Both have 20 gallons today.

  4. AVIONICS: OFF

    The avionics stay off for the start.

  5. ALTERNATE AIR: CLOSED

    Check the alternate air door is closed.

  6. THRUST LEVER: FULL

    Move the thrust lever all the way forward to check it moves freely. The engine is off, so nothing happens.

  7. THRUST LEVER: IDLE

    Now all the way back to IDLE. The diesel starts with the lever at idle: the FADEC does the rest.

  8. ENGINE MASTER: check OFF

    Still off. It comes on after the power and the strobe.

  9. ALTERNATOR: ON, then MASTER BATTERY: ON

    ALTR first, then BATT. The screens come on.

  10. FIN STROBE: ON

    Set the strobe switch to FIN. The tail strobe tells people outside the engine is about to start.

  11. FORCE B: AUTO

    The engine has two computers, FADEC A and FADEC B. In AUTO, A is in charge and B takes over if A fails. FORCE B is only for a test or a fault.

  12. Warning messages: CHECK

    Look at the lights under the engine page and the PFD messages. Press Next.

  13. Propeller area: CLEAR

    Look all round. At the school you follow the marshaller's signal. Press Next.

  14. ENGINE MASTER: ON

    The FADEC comes alive. With a cold engine the GLOW light comes on: the glow plugs are heating the cylinders.

  15. GLOW light: wait until it goes OUT

    A diesel has no spark plugs. It fires from the heat of compression, so a cold engine needs the glow plugs first. Do not press the starter until GLOW is out.

  16. ELECTRIC FUEL PUMP: ON

    The electric pump gives fuel pressure for the start.

  17. STARTER: hold until the engine starts

    Press and hold HOLD TO START. Let go as soon as the engine runs on its own and the RPM settles near 900.

  18. OIL PRESSURE: at least 1 bar

    The oil pressure must come up straight away. If it does not, switch the engine master OFF.

  19. ELECTRIC FUEL PUMP: OFF

    The engine-driven pump now does the job.

  20. BATTERY AMPS: positive

    BATT A must show a plus number: the alternator is charging the battery.

  21. VOLTS: in the green

    Bus volts about 28 with the alternator working.

  22. Note the start time (UTC)

    Write down the engine start time for the flight log. Press Next.
