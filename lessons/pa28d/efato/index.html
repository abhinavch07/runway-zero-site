PA-28 diesel · Lesson 8 · about 3 minutes

Emergency: engine failure after take-off

Share on WhatsApp

You are already flying with the engine running. The engine stops in the climb. Best glide 73 kt, land ahead, then fuel off, fuel pump off, engine master off, flaps, electrics off, door unlatched.

Start this lesson in the cockpitPA-28 diesel checklists

The steps

  1. Climbing out at 76 kt: watch the engine

    You are already flying with the engine running. Just after take-off, climbing on full power. The trainer flies the aircraft for you, wings level at the speed each step asks for. You do the actions.

  2. AIRSPEED: 73 kt (best glide)

    The engine has stopped. Lower the nose at once to keep flying speed: 73 kt with the flaps up, 65 kt with flaps. Watch the trainer lower the nose.

  3. Pick a field ahead

    Land straight ahead, turning no more than you need to miss obstacles. Do not turn back to the runway. Press Next.

  4. FUEL SELECTOR: OFF

    No fuel to feed a fire in the landing.

  5. ELECTRIC FUEL PUMP: OFF

    The pump would keep pushing fuel.

  6. ENGINE MASTER: OFF

    The FADEC and the engine off.

  7. FLAPS: as required

    Use flap to land short once you are sure of the field. Then 65 kt. Press Next.

  8. ALTERNATOR and BATTERY: OFF

    Electrics off before touchdown, to lower the fire risk.

  9. CABIN DOOR: unlatch before landing

    A bent door frame after a rough landing can jam the door shut. Unlatch it before touchdown. Press Next.
