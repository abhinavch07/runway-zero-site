7 drills

Air Regulations drills

  • Freedoms of the air: A flight is described. Name the freedom it uses.
  • Which convention?: Match each convention to what it deals with, both ways round.
  • Cruising levels: A track and flight rules are given. Pick the right cruising level under the semicircular rule.
  • VMC minima: A height and an airspace class are given. Pick the VMC visibility and cloud distance.
  • Light signals: Green, red or white, steady or flashing, in the air or on the ground. Say what the tower means.
  • Squawks, phases and calls: Transponder codes, alerting phases and emergency words, both ways round.
  • Read the PAPI: A PAPI picture is shown. Say where you are on the approach slope.
