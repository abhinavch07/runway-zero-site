Air Navigation drills

  • Departure: East-west distance along a parallel: departure = d.long (minutes) x cos latitude, and the other way round.
  • Convergency and conversion angle: Convergency = d.long x sin mean latitude. Conversion angle is half of it. Then great circle and rhumb line tracks.
  • True, magnetic and compass: Variation east, magnetic least. Deviation east, compass least. Convert headings both ways.
  • Arc, time, LMT and UTC: 15 degrees is one hour. LMT = UTC + longitude east. IST = UTC + 5:30.
  • Units of distance: Nautical miles, kilometres, statute miles, metres and feet.
  • Chart scale: Scale = chart length / Earth distance. 1 nm = 185,200 cm.
  • Wind triangle: From track, TAS and wind, find the heading to steer and the ground speed. Worked out exactly, as on the computer.
  • 1 in 60 corrections: Track error, closing angle and the turn to fly direct to destination.
  • Fuel sums: Volume to mass with specific gravity, gallons, fuel flow and endurance.
  • Glide path height and rate of descent: Height = slope x 100 x range. Rate of descent = GS x slope x 100 / 60.
  • Read the RMI and RBI: A picture of an RMI or an RBI. Find the QDM, the QDR or the relative bearing.
  • Read the VOR CDI: A picture of a VOR course deviation indicator. Which way to turn, or which radial you are on.
  • Read the HSI: A picture of a horizontal situation indicator. Find the heading, the course and where the course line is.
  • Centre of gravity: CG shift from moving a load, and CG as a percentage of MAC.
  • Critical point and PNR: CP = D x H / (O + H). PNR time = E x H / (O + H).
