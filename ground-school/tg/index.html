Technical General drills

  • Hydraulic jack force: Force = pressure x area.
  • Aquaplaning speed: Dynamic aquaplaning speed = 9 x √(tyre pressure in psi).
  • Ohm's law and power: V = I x R and P = V x I, plus resistors in series and parallel.
  • Logic gates: Two inputs go into a gate. Give the output.
  • Generator frequency: f = rpm x pole pairs / 60.
  • Compression ratio: CR = (swept volume + clearance volume) / clearance volume.
  • Which stroke? (picture): Look at the piston and the valves. Name the stroke.
  • Propeller blade angles: Blade angle = helix angle + angle of attack.
  • Propulsive efficiency: Propulsive efficiency = 2V / (V + Vj).
  • Gas turbine sections (picture): One section of a jet engine is marked X. Name it.
  • Lift, speed and weight: Lift goes with V². Speed for the same AoA goes with √weight.
  • Aerofoil terms (picture): One part of an aerofoil drawing is marked X. Name it.
  • High lift devices (picture): A wing section with a device fitted. Name the device.
  • Load factor and stall speed in a turn: n = 1 / cos(bank). Stall speed in the turn = Vs x √n.
  • Speed of sound and Mach number: a = 38.95 x √(T in K). Mach = TAS / a.
  • Rate of turn and radius: Rate one bank ≈ TAS/10 + 7. Radius goes with V² at a fixed bank.
  • Glide distance: Still air glide distance = height x L/D.
